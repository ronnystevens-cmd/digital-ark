02 · the one force
"Love is the only entropy that runs backward." — the force that defies the arrow of time

The universe falls apart and grows cold. Stars burn out, order drifts toward noise. And yet love does the impossible: it draws the scattered into a whole, returns warmth where warmth was lost, and heals what time would break. It is the only force that grows when it is shared — split a loaf and you have less; split love and somehow there is more.

Many believe God lives behind a glass ceiling — all-knowing, sharing every joy and every tear, but self-withdrawn to honour the gift of our free will. If God is within every human, then love is God. And when darkness falls, remember: it is only the absence of light.

"Darkness is only the absence of light. Love conquers all."
